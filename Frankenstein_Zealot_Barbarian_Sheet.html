The Modern Prometheus (1818 Novel)

Stitched together from dissecting rooms and charnel houses, you are 8 feet tall, superhumanly fast (40 ft speed), and self-educated on Paradise Lost and Plutarch's Lives. Though humanity recoils in horror from your stitched yellow visage (6 CHA), your mind is sharp (13 INT) and your strength is cataclysmic (20 STR).

Deathless Nature (Reborn)
  • No Breath, Food, or Sleep: Cannot be drowned, suffocated, or poisoned (Adv vs Poison & Poison Resistance).
  • Conscious Rest: Finishes a Long Rest in 4 hours sitting motionless with yellow eyes open.
  • Warrior of the Gods: Spells that restore you to life require zero material components.
