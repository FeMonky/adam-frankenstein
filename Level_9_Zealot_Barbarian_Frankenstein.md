# Adam ("The Creature") — Level 9 Path of the Zealot Barbarian

> *"I ought to be thy Adam, but I am rather the fallen angel, whom thou drivest from joy for no misdeed. Everywhere I see bliss, from which I alone am irrevocably excluded. I was benevolent and good; misery made me a fiend."*
> — **Mary Shelley, *Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus* (1818)**

---

## Character Overview & Horror Archetype
- **Name:** Adam *(or "The Creature", "The Modern Prometheus", "De Lacey's Pupil")*
- **Class & Level:** Barbarian 9 (*Path of the Zealot* — *Xanathar's Guide to Everything*)
- **Race / Lineage:** **Reborn** (*Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft*)
- **Background:** **Hermit / Scholar-Outcast** *(or Haunted One — customized from his winter hiding in the De Lacey family hovel reading Milton's Paradise Lost, Plutarch's Lives, and Goethe)*
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral *(Born benevolent; pushed to righteous, philosophical vengeance against cruel creators and true monsters)*
- **Horror Vibe:** **Mary Shelley's Original 1818 Literary Titan.** Standing 8 feet tall with translucent yellow skin stretched taut over giant arteries, stitched surgical seams, watery eyes, and flowing black hair. Unlike the slow, grunting pop-culture stereotype, Shelley's Creature is **superhumanly fast** (able to outrun horses and bound across vertical alpine glaciers), **articulate and deeply philosophical**, and **virtually impossible to kill**.

## Backstory & Roleplay Profile: *The Modern Prometheus*

### The Origin: Stitched from the Charnel Houses of Ingolstadt
You were not born; you were assembled on a rain-lashed November night from the largest bones of dissecting rooms and the butchered dead of charnel houses, jolted into galvanic life by a hubristic natural philosopher who took one look at your yellow, watery eyes and fled in terror.

Cast out into the freezing wilderness like a newborn titan, you learned the world through pain and secret observation. Hiding in a cramped woodshed beside the blind old man De Lacey's cottage, you taught yourself to speak, read, and reason from a leather portmanteau of books found in the snow: John Milton's *Paradise Lost*, Plutarch's *Lives*, and Victor's own laboratory journal detailing the sickening alchemy of your creation.

When humanity answered your pleas for companionship with musket fire, torches, and the destruction of your intended bride, you pursued your creator to the Arctic ice—and beyond. Now drawn into this nightmare realm, the heavy iron chains once meant to bind you are wrapped around your forearms as weapons. Whenever the galvanic lightning in your stitched heart surges, you quote Milton in a booming, sepulchral baritone while shattering the bones of monsters who prey on the innocent.

### Personality: The Eloquent Titan (`13 INT` / `6 CHA`)
- **The `13 INT` vs. `6 CHA` Paradox:** Your mind is sharp, poetic, and well-read—you speak in formal 19th-century prose, debating morality, theology, and anatomy. Yet your `6 CHA (-2)` reflects the visceral, instinctive terror your 8-foot stitched corpse-frame inspires in mortals before you even open your mouth *(which is why you use your **20 Strength** for **Intimidation +9** via the PHB p. 175 *Variant: Skills with Different Abilities* rule—lifting a man off the floor by his collar or snapping an iron poker in half while speaking calmly!).*
- **Galvanic Memory Flashes (*Knowledge from a Past Life*):** Your brain and hands were stitched together from multiple dead men—a master surgeon, a dockworker, an alpine mountaineer, a soldier. Four times per day, muscle memory from one of your donors' past lives surges through your sutures, adding **`+1d6`** to your skill checks.
- **Roleplay Traits:**
  - **Personality Trait 1:** *"I speak with formal, melancholic eloquence—even when holding a cultist by the throat three feet off the cobblestones."*
  - **Personality Trait 2:** *"Having no need to sleep, eat, or breathe, I spend the night hours standing motionless in the rain or reading by candlelight while the living rest."*
  - **Ideal:** *"Reckoning. Those who create monsters—or abuse their power over the weak—must answer for what they have wrought."*
  - **Bond:** *"A water-stained, blood-spattered copy of Milton's Paradise Lost and the pages of Victor's laboratory journal sewn inside my coat."*
  - **Flaw:** *"Beneath my stoic philosophy lies an abyss of rejection; open cruelty or the sight of torches and mobs can trigger a volcanic galvanic rage."*

## Complete Feature Breakdown (Reborn + Zealot Barbarian 9)

### Racial Traits: Reborn (*Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft*)
- **Deathless Nature (Constructed Corpse):**
  - You have **Advantage on saving throws against disease and being poisoned**, and you have **Resistance to Poison damage**.
  - You have **Advantage on Death Saving Throws**.
  - You **don't need to eat, drink, or breathe** (you cannot be drowned or suffocated by gas!).
  - You **don't need to sleep**, and magic can't put you to sleep. You can finish a Long Rest in **4 hours** if you spend those hours in an inactive, motionless state, during which you retain full consciousness (watching the room with your yellow eyes open).
- **Knowledge from a Past Life (`4 uses / Long Rest`):** When you make an ability check that uses a skill, you can roll **`1d6`** immediately after seeing the number on the d20 and add it to the check.

### Barbarian Core Features (Levels 1–9)
- **Galvanic Rage (`4 / Long Rest`, Bonus Action):**
  - **Resistance** to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
  - **Advantage** on Strength checks and Strength saving throws.
  - **`+3` Rage Damage** on all melee Strength attacks (increases to +3 at Level 9!).
- **Reckless Attack (Lvl 2):** On your first attack on your turn, you can decide to attack recklessly—giving yourself **Advantage on all melee Strength attack rolls** during this turn, while attacks against you have Advantage until your next turn.
- **Danger Sense (Lvl 2):** **Advantage on Dexterity saving throws** against effects you can see (fireballs, lightning bolts, traps).
- **Extra Attack & Fast Movement (Lvl 5):** Attack **twice** whenever you take the Attack action, and your walking speed increases to **40 ft.**
- **Feral Instinct (Lvl 7):** You have **Advantage on Initiative rolls**. Additionally, if you are surprised at the beginning of combat and aren't incapacitated, you can act normally on your first turn if you enter your Rage before doing anything else.
- **Brutal Critical (Lvl 9 Feature):** You can roll **one additional weapon damage die** when determining the extra damage for a critical hit with a melee attack (e.g. Maul rolls `5d6` on a crit!).

### Path of the Zealot Subclass Features (Levels 3, 6)
*Flavor: The alchemical lightning that animated your stitched body refuses to let your heart stop beating, discharging in blinding galvanic arcs when you strike.*

- **Divine Fury — "Galvanic Discharge" (Lvl 3):** While you're raging, the first creature you hit on each of your turns with a weapon attack takes extra damage equal to **`1d6 + 4`** (`1d6 + half your barbarian level`, rounded down: half of 9 is 4). You choose whether this damage is **Radiant** *(blinding galvanic lightning)* or **Necrotic** *(charnel-house rot)*.
- **Warrior of the Gods — "Stitched for Reanimation" (Lvl 3):** Your body is a conduit for reanimation. Any spell or effect that restores you to life (like *Revivify* or *Raise Dead*) requires **zero material components**—a jolt of lightning and a needle and thread are enough!
- **Fanatical Focus — "Unstoppable Titan" (Lvl 6):** Once per Rage, if you fail a saving throw (such as *Hold Person*, *Fear*, or a dragon's breath), you can **reroll the saving throw**, and you must use the new roll!
- *(Note: Zealous Presence unlocks at Level 10).*

## Attacks & Damage Breakdown (While Raging)

### 1. The Shipyard Anchor-Chain / Iron Maul (`2d6` Bludgeoning, Heavy, Two-Handed)
- **Standard Attack (`2x per turn`):** **+9 to hit** *(almost always with **Advantage** via Reckless Attack)*.
  - **First Hit on Turn (with Galvanic Discharge):** `2d6 + 8` bludgeoning *(+5 STR, +3 Rage)* + `1d6 + 4` Radiant/Necrotic *(Avg: **22.5 damage**).*
  - **Second Hit on Turn:** `2d6 + 8` bludgeoning *(Avg: **15 damage**).*
- **Great Weapon Master Power Smash (`-5 to Hit / +10 Damage`):** **+4 to hit** *(with **Reckless Advantage**, equivalent to ~+9 to hit!)*.
  - **First GWM Hit on Turn:** `2d6 + 18` bludgeoning + `1d6 + 4` Galvanic Discharge *(Avg: **32.5 damage** on a single swing!).*
  - **Second GWM Hit on Turn:** `2d6 + 18` bludgeoning *(Avg: **25 damage**).*
  - **Full Turn GWM Combo (2 Hits):** **`4d6 + 36` bludgeoning + `1d6 + 4` Galvanic = Average `57.5 damage` per turn!**
  - **GWM Bonus Action Cleave:** Whenever you score a **Critical Hit** or **reduce a creature to 0 HP**, you can make a **3rd melee weapon attack as a Bonus Action**!
  - **Brutal Critical Hit:** On a crit with the Maul, roll **`5d6 + 8`** (or **`5d6 + 18`** with GWM)!

### 2. Bare-Handed Throat-Crush & Improvised Weapons (Tombstones, Doors, Pews)
- **Attack Bonus:** **+9 to hit** (with Advantage via Reckless Attack).
- **Damage:** `1d4 + 8` bludgeoning (or `1d8 + 8` / `2d6 + 8` for heavy improvised objects like a ripped-off oak door or granite headstone) + `1d6 + 4` Galvanic Discharge.
- **Bonus Action Grapple (Tavern Brawler):** Immediately after hitting a creature with an Unarmed Strike or Improvised Weapon on your turn, use a **Bonus Action** to **Grapple** them (`+9 Athletics` with **Advantage** while Raging), locking them in your iron grip (Speed 0)!
  - **The "Frankenstein Choke-Slam" Combo:** Hit with Attack 1 (or Shove them **Prone** so they can't stand up because their speed is 0 while Grappled!), Grapple as a Bonus Action, and pummel them on the ground while they have Disadvantage on all attacks against you!
